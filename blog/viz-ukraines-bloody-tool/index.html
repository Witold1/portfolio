Officially reported casualties in Russia–Ukraine war, since Feb 2022

Visualize official casualty reports from the active stage of the Russia–Ukraine war using PyData, Selenium, BeautifulSoup4, and LLM-assisted extraction.

Notes on interpretation

What’s important to understand before reading the numbers?
  1. These are casualties, not fatalities. A “casualty” can include killed, wounded, captured, missing, etc., and can be counted multiple times across time depending on reporting practices.
  2. These are cross-reported figures (Country A reporting Country B losses, and vice-versa). They are best thought of as official propaganda signals rather than ground truth.
  3. Quantification is uncertain; a plausible “ground truth” could be far from cross-reports for any given day.
  4. You can apply your own wounded-to-killed ratios if you want to reason about fatalities, but assumptions will dominate.
