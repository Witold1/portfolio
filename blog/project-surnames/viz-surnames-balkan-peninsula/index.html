Surnames of the Balkan Peninsula (merged region)

Surname frequency wordclouds for the Balkan Peninsula, by administrative unit. Political boundaries are shown according to their “de facto” status. Data came from Forebears.io.

Hub: Project “Surnames of the World”.

How to read these wordcloud maps?▾
Click or tap to open section

The bigger the word, the more frequent the surname. Wordclouds are built from aggregated surname frequencies by administrative unit.
