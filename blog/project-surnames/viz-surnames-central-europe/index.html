Surnames of Central Europe (merged region)

Surname frequency wordclouds for Central Europe, by administrative unit. Political boundaries are shown according to their “de facto” status. Data came from Forebears.io.

How to read these wordcloud maps?▾
Click or tap to open section

The bigger the word, the more frequent the surname. Wordclouds are built from aggregated surname frequencies by administrative unit.
