## Description

See interactive chart by [LINK - "How American Is Your Car?"](https://witold1.github.io/cars-made-in-usa/). 

Check vehicles distributed by share of American components, from 0% to 100%, across filters: vehicle type, manufacturing corporation, and region of those corporations. Currently for selected model years only. 
For best experience, open with your personal computer or laptop.
