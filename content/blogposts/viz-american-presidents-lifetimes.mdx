## What is this about?

  An interactive recreation of the well-known *Roman Emperors* circular lifetime chart by [@dataisinteresting’s WRE-Emperors](https://github.com/dataisinteresting/WRE-Emperors), applied to U.S. presidents.

  Related earlier work on this site: [Rulers of the Kazakh Khanate](/blog/viz-rulers-of-kazakh-khanate).

## How does it work?

  Each president is one glyph.

  A thin white arc shows lifespan (full circle = 100 years), starting at birth (12 o’clock) and running clockwise.
  A thick red arc shows time served in office (Cleveland and Trump show two segments for non-consecutive terms).
  An icon at the arc’s end marks manner of death, or a living marker for presidents still alive.

  Sort, filter by manner of death, switch theme, and export SVG/PNG from the page chrome.

## Try it yourself

  See the source code now on [GitHub](https://github.com/Witold1/rulers-lifetimes-and-reign-arcs).

## Data

JSON under `data/` is the source of truth:

* `meta.json` - “as of” date for living presidents’ open arcs
* `presidents.json` - one object per unique president

No bundler: serve `index.html` over HTTP so ES modules and `fetch` work. See the repo’s `CONTRIBUTING.md` for corrections.
