## What is this about?

This post is a spin-off from ["Surnames of the world"](../../projects/project-surnames-navigator/), to explore the most common forenames across the various regions of the world through wordcloud visualisation. 
Political boundaries shown according to their ["de facto" status](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/disputed-boundaries-policy). Data came from [Forebears.io](https://forebears.io/).

Read more about local naming customs at [Familysearch.org's wiki](https://www.familysearch.org/en/wiki/Main_Page). 
Also, for some countries and regions you can find academic papers that explain how names were forming there.
