## Method ?

The chart is a **small multiple** (grid chart):

- Extract all mapped buildings within each city boundary.
- Count exterior vertices (nodes) for each building polygon (inner courtyards/patios not explicitly counted).
- Plot the 100 buildings with the most nodes.

Key caveats:

1. Under this methodology, “interesting” buildings can be repetitive due to construction patterns (housing projects, industrial structures, etc.).
2. Some truly complex structures aren’t mapped well and require case-by-case handling (multi-buildings, connections, etc.).
